Mobile app · Social game

Wexy

A wedding game built around photo and video missions, a live leaderboard and a shared album.

Project details

Wexy turns the wedding into a shared game. The couple sets up an event and invites guests to join by link or code. Guests complete photo and video missions and share what they capture in the event feed.

A live leaderboard tracks points during the event. The shared album brings the photos and videos together afterwards.

App StoreGoogle Play

Mobile app · Photo & video

Wexy Album

A dedicated app for collecting, browsing and saving photos and videos from a wedding.

Project details

The couple and their guests can open their shared album, browse photos and videos in a grid and save selected media to their devices.

The app supports batch uploads and shows progress while the files upload and videos are processed.
